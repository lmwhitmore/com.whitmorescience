      <p class="ws-eyebrow">Laura M. Whitmore</p>
      <h2>Investigating the connections between oceans, ice, and margins.</h2>
      <p>My research centers on Arctic Ocean dynamics by using trace elements and nutrients as indicators of circulation and biogeochemical processes. I am especially interested in how sea ice, rivers, sediments, and broad continental shelves influence the chemistry of the open ocean.</p>
      <p>This work has taken me from the Northern Gulf of Mexico and equatorial Pacific to the Arctic Ocean and Antarctica's Amundsen Sea. Across settings, I value careful sampling, transparent analysis, reusable scientific tools, and friendly collaboration.</p>
